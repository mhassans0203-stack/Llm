## 3. Culinary Craftsmanship & Ingredient Standards

Every pizza at Forno Antico follows strict artisan standards:

1. **48-Hour Cold Sourdough Fermentation**:
   * Made exclusively with Italian **Caputo Tipo 00** wheat flour, pure mountain water, sea salt from Trapani, and the Bellini family’s 40-year-old sourdough mother starter (*criscito*).
   * Result: A light, cloud-like, highly digestible crust (*cornicione*) with zero commercial yeast bloating.
2. **Mount Vesuvius Volcanic Stone Wood-Fired Oven**:
   * Custom-built by third-generation Neapolitan stone masons using porous volcanic refractory stone from Mount Vesuvius.
   * Fueled exclusively by seasoned oak, beech, and olive wood.
   * Maintains a constant internal temperature of **450°C (842°F)**.
   * Bakes each pizza in exactly **90 seconds**, producing a blistered, leopard-spotted char while keeping toppings succulent.
3. **Certified D.O.P. Ingredients Imported from Campania**:
   * **San Marzano D.O.P. Tomatoes**: Grown exclusively in the fertile volcanic soil beneath Mount Vesuvius in Agro Sarnese-Nocerino; hand-crushed with sea salt.
   * **Mozzarella di Bufala Campana D.O.P.**: Fresh creamy buffalo milk mozzarella air-freighted twice weekly from Caserta.
   * **Fior di Latte d'Agerola**: Delicate, clean cow's milk mozzarella that melts without greasiness.
   * **Prosciutto di Parma D.O.P.**: Cured for 24 months in Langhirano, sliced paper-thin.
   * **Parmigiano-Reggiano D.O.P.**: Aged for a full 24 months for rich crystalline umami.
   * **Extra Virgin Olive Oil**: Cold-pressed monocultivar Sicilian Nocellara del Belice.
4. **Sustainability & Eco-Commitment**:
   * Zero artificial preservatives, zero additives, zero GMOs.
   * 100% compostable, unbleached plant-fiber takeaway and delivery boxes.

## 9. Dietary, Allergy & Health Guidelines

* **Gluten Sensitivity / Celiac Disease**:
  * We offer an artisan **Gluten-Free Cauliflower & Cassava Crust** prepared in a dedicated sanitised station.
  * *Important Notice*: Because we handle traditional wheat flour in our open kitchen, airborne flour dust may be present. Highly sensitive celiac guests should exercise personal discretion.
* **Vegetarian Dining**:
  * Margherita D.O.P., Ortolana del Giardino, Tartufo & Funghi, Quattro Formaggi, and Cinque Formaggi are 100% vegetarian friendly.
* **Dairy-Free & Vegan Accommodations**:
  * Any pizza can be prepared *senza formaggio* (without cheese) in the traditional Marinara style with extra San Marzano sauce, fresh garlic, oregano, and basil.
  * Vegan cheese substitutions are available upon request.
* **Nut Allergies**:
  * The *Quattro Formaggi Nobile* contains crushed toasted walnuts.
  * The *Gamberi & Pesto Genovese* contains pine nuts.
  * Kitchen surfaces handling nuts are kept separate.
* **Pork-Free Options**:
  * All non-meat pizzas, plus chicken and seafood options, are 100% pork-free. All cured meats (Prosciutto, Salami, Soppressata, 'Nduja, Pancetta) are strictly Italian pork products.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for the Chatbot

#### Q1: What makes Neapolitan pizza different from New York or Chicago style pizza?
> **Answer**: True Neapolitan pizza has a soft, light, airy, and blistered crust (*cornicione*) caused by 48 hours of natural sourdough fermentation. It is flash-baked in 90 seconds at 450°C (842°F) in a wood-fired oven. It is tender, slightly smoky, and topped with simple, high-purity Italian ingredients like San Marzano D.O.P. tomatoes and fresh buffalo mozzarella.

#### Q2: What is the most popular pizza on your menu?
> **Answer**: Our top bestsellers are the **Margherita D.O.P.** (our classic benchmark), the **Prosciutto di Parma & Burrata** (decadent and fresh), and the **Diavola Piccante** (spicy Calabrian salami with hot honey and 'Nduja).

#### Q3: How do I get free delivery?
> **Answer**: Delivery is completely free on all orders of $40 or more! For orders under $40, we charge a flat fee of $3.99.

#### Q4: Do you have promo codes or first-time customer discounts?
> **Answer**: Yes! You can use promo code **`FORNO20`** at checkout for **20% off** your order. If you join our VIP Club newsletter at the bottom of our website, you will also receive an instant $10 voucher code (`VIVAITALIA10`).

#### Q5: Can I customize my pizza with different crusts or toppings?
> **Answer**: Absolutely! You can select between 12" and 16" sizes, choose our Classic Sourdough, Gluten-Free, or Stuffed Mozzarella crust, adjust your spice level, and add premium toppings like Black Truffle Glaze, Extra Buffalo Mozzarella, Hot Honey, or Spicy 'Nduja.

#### Q6: How do I book a table?
> **Answer**: You can click the **"Book a Table"** button in our website header, or let me assist you right here! We offer seating in our Main Dining Hall, Heated Garden Veranda, Chef's Counter, or Private Wine Room.

#### Q7: Where are you located and what are your opening hours?
> **Answer**: We are located at **482 Via Bellini Boulevard** in the Little Italy District (Naples Square). We are open Monday–Thursday 12 PM–10:30 PM, Friday–Saturday 12 PM–11:30 PM, and Sunday 12 PM–10 PM. Delivery runs daily from 11:30 AM to 11:00 PM.
